Comparison to $f$-term

The most natural comparison of results that can be done is to the phase diagram of the $f$-interacting Majorana chain model. Being the only other five site quartic Majorana interaction, we can expect that the phase diagrams would appear similar to one another. The biggest difference between the two phase diagrams is the nature of the transition between the gapped AF/PM phase to the Floating phase above.
