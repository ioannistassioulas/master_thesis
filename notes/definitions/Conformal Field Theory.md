## Defintion
A conformal field theory is a quantum field theory that is specifically invariant under conformal transformations. Conformal Transformations are transformations that are locally equivalent to a rotation and a dilation - it does not affect the angles of two intersecting curves despite space dilation i.e. the conformal group preserves angles.
