## Definition

A floating phase in the context of quantum phase transitions describes a critical phase (i.e. gapless phase) with power-law correlations that *vary*.

One important aspect of floating phases is the ***incommensurability*** of the correlations - this means that the wavevector that describes the order parameter is no longer periodic with the lattice size and it varies continuously.
