## Definition
Also known as an electronic topological transition. This is a change in the topology of a Fermi surface without symmetry breaking. This is a second order phase transition!

## Examples
Doping iron-based superconductors - as you dope more and more vacuums inside, the electrons vanish leading to a topological transition without breaking symmetry.
